# Blog

Articles use the Astro Content Layer from `content/blog`. The editable landing documents are `content/blog/{locale}/_index.mdx`; project mother documents and child articles live below `content/blog/{locale}/projects/{project-id}/`. The blog index, category archives, and articles are nested below `/{locale}/blog/`; the blog has no section progress navigator. The listing derives categories, tags, favorite project hubs, and recent articles from metadata. The reader adds navigation, Table of Contents, Mermaid, Markmap, and print styles.

Project articles live at `content/blog/{locale}/projects/{project-id}/{article-slug}.mdx`. Every visible project has a published bilingual `overview.mdx` with matching `category: projects` and `subcategory: <project-id>`; its homepage, Projects-index card, and Blog favorite hub link directly to this reading route. The overview renders its derived child-article list. Use `npm run content:new -- blog <locale> projects/<project-id> <article-slug>` for supplementary drafts. Existing general topic folders remain supported. Article links, translations, RSS, canonical URLs, project breadcrumbs, and project-scoped previous/next navigation are generated from the nested article ID.
