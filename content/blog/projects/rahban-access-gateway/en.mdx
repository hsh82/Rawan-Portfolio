Rahban is the focused project hub for a browser-based remote-access gateway. The original work centers on integrating identity, secrets, virtual-machine management, interface and PWA behavior, and reproducible Docker delivery around the upstream RustGuac and guacd core. It remains an internal pilot rather than a claim of production-hardened remote-desktop infrastructure.
